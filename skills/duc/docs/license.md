At `Ducflair` we envisioned a future where the `duc file format` is widely adopted and utilized across a variety of industries and applications. By licensing it under the [`MIT License`](https://github.com/ducflair/duc/blob/main/LICENSE), we’re ensuring that `duc` can be freely used, modified, and distributed by anyone, for any purpose, without fear of legal repercussions.
All of that to say that we are in favor to:

> **Support Innovative Minds that want to push us forward into a greater future**.

Whether you’re a developer looking to integrate the Duc file format into your software, an engineer interested in exploring its capabilities, or a contributor eager to improve upon its foundations, the MIT License ensures that you can do so with confidence and clarity regarding intellectual property and rights.
